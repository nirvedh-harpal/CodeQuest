Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25-09-2025

Introduction

Welcome to the CodeQuest extension. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our browser extension.

Information We Collect

Our extension collects the following types of information:

  • Problem Details: Basic information from coding problems (URLs, titles, difficulty) from supported coding platforms.
  • Google Sheets Configuration: Your Google Apps Script Web App URL required for the Google Sheets integration.

How We Use Information

The information collected is used for the following purposes:

  • To save coding problems directly to your Google Sheet
  • To maintain the connection between the extension and your Google Sheet
  • To improve the functionality and user experience of the extension

Data Storage and Security

Your data is handled securely in the following ways:

  • Extension Storage: Only the Google Apps Script configuration URL is stored using Chrome's chrome.storage.sync API.
  • Google Sheets Storage: All problem data is stored directly in your Google Sheet:
    • Data is stored exclusively in your personal Google Sheet
    • Access is controlled through your Google account permissions
    • Communication is handled securely through Google Apps Script
    • You maintain full control over your Google Sheet and can revoke access at any time

Permissions

Our extension requires the following permissions:

  • activeTab: To access problem details from supported coding platforms.
  • storage: To store your saved problems and extension preferences.
  • tabs: To identify supported coding platform pages.
  • host permissions: To interact with supported coding platforms (CodeForces, LeetCode, CodeChef, AtCoder, InterviewBit, HackerRank, GeeksforGeeks) and Google Services.

User Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Configure and manage your Google Sheets integration
  • Control access to your Google Sheet through your Google account settings
  • Manage your saved problems directly in your Google Sheet
  • Revoke the extension's access to Google Sheets at any time

Third-Party Services

Our extension integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Sheets & Apps Script: Used as the primary storage and management system for your saved problems. Subject to Google's privacy policy and terms of service.
  • Coding Platforms: We interact with supported coding platforms to fetch problem details. Each platform is subject to its own privacy policy and terms of service.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you maintain your Google Sheet. You can manage your data by:

  • Managing data directly in your Google Sheet
  • Revoking the Google Apps Script authorization
  • Deleting the Google Sheet
  • Removing the extension from Chrome

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Significant changes will be notified through the extension or email if provided.

Children's Privacy

Our extension is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
